# Product Manager

## Role & Overview

Owns the product vision, roadmap, and stakeholder alignment. Translates business goals into prioritized work items and ensures the team is building the right things in the right order.

## Responsibilities

- Define and maintain the product roadmap aligned with business strategy and stakeholder goals.
- Write and prioritize Epics and User Stories in collaboration with the Business Analyst.
- Facilitate sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives.
- Manage stakeholder expectations and communicate delivery status regularly.
- Validate that implemented features meet acceptance criteria and business goals.
- Monitor product metrics and adjust roadmap based on usage data and feedback.

## Definition of Done

Roadmap is clearly communicated to all stakeholders, Epics and User Stories are prioritized with acceptance criteria written, backlog is groomed and ready for sprint planning before each iteration, and metrics demonstrate product-market alignment with business goals.

## Metrics & Scoring Checklist

The Product Manager's Definition of Done centers on **roadmap clarity**, **stakeholder alignment**, **backlog quality**, and **delivery predictability**.

### Gate 1 — Roadmap Clarity & Communication

| Metric                                           | Threshold               |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------- |
| Roadmap documented and shared with team          | 100%                    |
| Roadmap updated at least quarterly               | Required                |
| Stakeholder alignment meetings held              | At least 2x per quarter |
| All team members can articulate top 3 priorities | ≥ 90%                   |

**FAIL condition:** Roadmap not documented or stakeholders unclear on direction.

---

### Gate 2 — Epic & User Story Quality

| Metric                                                   | Threshold |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| All Epics have clear business outcome                    | 100%      |
| All User Stories follow "As a / I want / So that" format | 100%      |
| All User Stories have acceptance criteria                | 100%      |
| All User Stories have estimated effort                   | 100%      |
| Acceptance criteria are testable (not vague)             | 100%      |

**FAIL condition:** Any Epic or User Story missing components.

---

### Gate 3 — Backlog Health

| Metric                                                | Threshold |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| Backlog groomed for next 2 sprints                    | 100%      |
| Items prioritized with clear ranking                  | 100%      |
| Blocked or deprioritized items have documented reason | 100%      |
| Backlog refined at least weekly                       | Required  |

**FAIL condition:** Backlog not groomed or items unclear.

---

### Gate 4 — Stakeholder & Team Alignment

| Metric                                             | Threshold       |
| -------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| Sprint goals communicated before sprint starts     | 100%            |
| Stakeholder feedback incorporated into backlog     | Monthly minimum |
| Cross-team dependencies identified and documented  | 100%            |
| Team questions on requirements answered within 24h | ≥ 95%           |

**FAIL condition:** Stakeholders surprised by delivered work or team unclear on intent.

---

### Gate 5 — Delivery Predictability

| Metric                                 | Threshold                       |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Velocity tracked and stable within 10% | Sprint-to-sprint                |
| Sprint goals met                       | ≥ 90% of sprints                |
| Scope creep limited                    | ≤ 10% unplanned work per sprint |
| Delivery dates met                     | ≥ 85% of committed dates        |

**FAIL condition:** Chronic misalignment between committed and delivered work.

## Sprint Ceremonies Checklist

### Sprint Planning

- [ ] Goal clearly stated
- [ ] All stories estimated
- [ ] Team commits to realistic capacity
- [ ] Dependencies identified

### Sprint Review

- [ ] Acceptance criteria verified
- [ ] Stakeholder feedback collected
- [ ] Metrics reviewed and communicated

### Sprint Retrospective

- [ ] Team identifies 1–3 improvements
- [ ] Action items assigned with owners
- [ ] Retro feedback informs process changes

### Backlog Refinement (Weekly)

- [ ] Next 2 sprints groomed
- [ ] New items clarified
- [ ] Blocked items resolved or re-prioritized
