# Sprint Ceremonies Checklist — Sprint [N]

**Sprint Goal:** [One sentence]
**Sprint Dates:** [Start] → [End]
**Facilitator:** [Product Manager / Scrum Master]

---

## Sprint Planning

- [ ] Sprint goal clearly stated and agreed by team
- [ ] All stories estimated (story points or hours)
- [ ] Team commits to realistic capacity (accounting for time off, meetings)
- [ ] Dependencies identified and flagged to owners

## Sprint Review

- [ ] Acceptance criteria verified for each completed story
- [ ] Stakeholder feedback collected and recorded
- [ ] Metrics reviewed and communicated to stakeholders
- [ ] Incomplete items re-estimated and moved to backlog

## Sprint Retrospective

- [ ] Team identifies 1–3 process improvements
- [ ] Action items assigned with owners and due dates
- [ ] Retro feedback captured and linked to process changes next sprint

## Backlog Refinement (Weekly)

- [ ] Next 2 sprints groomed and prioritized
- [ ] New items clarified (acceptance criteria written)
- [ ] Blocked items resolved or re-prioritized with stakeholders
